# A Lighter, More Flavorful Sangria

## Preparation time
Start to finish: 10 minutes, plus chilling

## Servings
Serves 6

## Ingredients
- ¼ cup white sugar
- 8 large basil leaves
- 750-milliliter bottle white vinho verde, chilled
- 2 large celery stalks, cut on the diagonal into 1-inch pieces
- Two 2-inch cinnamon sticks
- 3 tablespoons orange juice, plus ½ orange, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice, plus ½ lemon, thinly sliced
- 1 peach or nectarine, halved, pitted and sliced
- 2 cups chilled seltzer or soda water

## Instructions
1. In a large pitcher or punch bowl, stir together the sugar, basil until the sugar dissolves. Stir in the wine, celery, cinnamon, both juices and the peach slices. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour or up to overnight. Just before serving, stir in the citrus slices and seltzer. Serve over ice.

## Cuisine name inferred from recipe
Spanish

## Tips
- Use a crisp, refreshing white vinho verde, pinto gris or unoaked chardonnay for this recipe.
- Add the citrus slices just before serving to impart brightness and a slight effervescence without bitterness.
- For a non-alcoholic sangria, replace the wine with an equal amount of chilled grape juice, pomegranate juice or apple cider.
