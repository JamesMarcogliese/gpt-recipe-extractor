# Annatto Oil (Makes ¾ cup)

## Preparation Time
Not provided specifically for annatto oil.

## Servings
Makes ¾ cup

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon annatto seeds
- ¾ cup grapeseed or other neutral oil

## Instructions
1. In a small saucepan, combine the annatto seeds and oil. Heat over low, occasionally swirling the pan, until the oil is deep red, about 10 minutes. Cool completely, then pour through a fine-mesh strainer set over a jar. Cover and store at room temperature for several months.

## Cuisine Name Inferred from Recipe
Puerto Rican

## Tips
Please ensure coolance before straining and storing the oil.
