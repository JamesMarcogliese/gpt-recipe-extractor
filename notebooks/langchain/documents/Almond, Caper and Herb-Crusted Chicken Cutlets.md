# Almond, Caper and Herb-Crusted Chicken Cutlets

## Preparation time
Start to finish: 25 minutes

## Servings
Servings: 4

## Ingredients
- ¾ cup sliced almonds, finely chopped
- ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
- Kosher salt and ground black pepper
- Four 4-ounce chicken cutlets (about ¼ inch thick)
- ¼ cup drained capers, finely chopped
- ¼ cup lightly packed fresh tarragon or fresh dill, chopped
- 6 tablespoons neutral oil, divided
- Lemon wedges or Dijon mustard or sour cream, to serve

## Instructions
1. Combine the almonds, panko, 1 teaspoon salt and ½ teaspoon pepper. Season the cutlets on both sides with salt and pepper, then sprinkle with the capers and tarragon, pressing to adhere.
2. Coat both sides of each cutlet with the almond mixture, pressing firmly. In a 12-inch skillet, heat 3 tablespoons of oil.
3. Add 2 cutlets and cook until golden brown on both sides.
4. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and wipe out the skillet. Repeat with remaining cutlets and oil.
5. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Cuisine name inferred from recipe
Mediterranean

## Tips
For an accompaniment, a lemony arugula salad or roasted sweet potatoes are suggested. Alternatively, sandwich the cutlets and baby greens in bread smeared with mayonnaise.
