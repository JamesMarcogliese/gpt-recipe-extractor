# Any Noodle Can Be Ramen!

## Preparation time
Start to finish: 35 minutes

## Servings
4

## Ingredients
- 8 ounces non-instant or dried ramen noodles OR 8 to 12 ounces precooked ramen noodles OR 10 ounces vermicelli pasta cooked according to the instructions in headnote
- 2 teaspoons neutral oil
- ¾ cup soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons neutral oil
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- Ground black pepper
- 8 ounces bacon, chopped
- 2 medium shallots, halved and thinly sliced
- 1 medium red bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, and thinly sliced
- 4 cups green cabbage, cut into 1-inch pieces (about 2 cups)
- Roasted seaweed snacks, cut into thin strips, to serve
- Pickled ginger, to serve

## Instructions
1. If using dried ramen, in a large pot, bring 4 quarts water to a boil. Add the ramen and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, then drain in a colander. Rinse under cool running water until cool to the touch, then drain again. Drizzle with the oil and toss to coat; set aside. If using precooked ramen, rinse the noodles in cold water; set aside. If using vermicelli, separate the strands with your fingers. Drizzle with the oil and toss to coat; set aside. If using ramenized Italian pasta, drizzle with oil and toss to coat; set aside.
2. In a small bowl, stir together the sauce, water, and ½ teaspoon pepper.
3. In a 12-inch skillet over medium-high, cook the bacon, stirring occasionally, until beginning to crisp and brown, 4 to 6 minutes. Add the shallots, bell pepper, and cabbage. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the shallots are lightly browned and the peppers and cabbage are just wilted, 5 to 8 minutes.
4. Add the noodles and soy sauce mixture. Cook over medium, contin uously stirring, to toss and turn, until the pork is dry, 3 to 5 minutes. Serve sprin kled with the nori and with pickle ginger on the side.

## Cuisine name inferred from recipe
Japanese cuisine, as this dish uses elements like soy sauce, seaweed, and pickled ginger which are commonly found in many Japanese dishes.

## Tips
- The recipe suggests that virtually any noodle can be transformed into "ramen" by cooking it and adding flavors typical of a ramen dish.
- A key tip is to ensure that the noodles are cooled and well-drained before adding the other ingredients to prevent sogginess.
