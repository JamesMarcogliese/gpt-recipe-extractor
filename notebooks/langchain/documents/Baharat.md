# Baharat

## Preparation time
Start to finish: 5 minutes

## Servings
Makes about 1/4 cup

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons packed brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cardamom
- 1 tablespoon ground coriander
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon ground sumac
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon granulated garlic

## Instructions
- In a small bowl, whisk together all ingredients.
- Transfer to an airtight container and store in a cool, dry place for up to a few months.

## Cuisine name inferred from recipe
Middle Eastern Spice Blend

## Tips
The baharat spice mix can be used to season dishes ranging from meats to vegetables and is especially well-suited for flavorful Middle Eastern dishes. It can also be added to recipes in small amounts to introduce a complex flavor profile.
