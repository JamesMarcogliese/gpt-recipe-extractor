# Afghan-Style Chickpea and Tomato Curry

## Preparation time
Not specified in the image

## Servings
Each Tuesday Night recipe makes four servings.

## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons grapeseed or other neutral oil
- 1 large red onion, finely chopped
- 2 medium garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 tablespoon curry powder
- ½ teaspoon ground cardamom
- Three 15½-ounce cans chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 1 teaspoon white sugar
- 1 pound ripe tomatoes, cored and chopped, divided
- Kosher salt and ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons white vinegar
- ½ cup lightly packed fresh flat-leaf parsley

## Instructions
In a 12-inch skillet over medium-high, heat the oil until shimmering. Add three-quarters of the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and beginning to brown, 5 to 7 minutes. Add the garlic, bay, curry powder and cardamom, then cook, stirring, until fragrant, 30 to 60 seconds.

Stir in the chickpeas, sugar, all but about ½ cup of the tomatoes, 1 cup water and ½ teaspoon each salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook, uncovered and stirring occasionally, until the sauce is thickened and clings to the chickpeas, 8 to 10 minutes.

Off heat, stir in the vinegar, then taste and season with salt and pepper. Remove and discard the bay. Transfer to a serving bowl and top with the remaining tomatoes, remaining onion and parsley.
